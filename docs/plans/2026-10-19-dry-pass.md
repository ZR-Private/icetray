## Global Constraints

- Read `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `AGENTS.md` first; they bind every task.
- Behavior-preserving: same results, errors and messages, metrics and labels, wire, on-disk formats (no `CHUNK_FILE_VERSION` or `VERIFIER_VERSION` bump unless the bytes change, and they must not), and the same costs: GETs, bytes, rounds, and allocations per op on the bench's hot paths.
- Tests stay as strong: no assertion weakened, no case dropped; a test that moves keeps its name or is renamed to `test_<fn>_<scenario>`.
- Take a finding only if it survives a fresh read; drop or shrink one that does not, and say so in the report. Do not rename for taste.
- Run cargo only through flox. Gate per task: `flox activate -- dev/ci-checks.sh` and `flox activate -- cargo test --workspace --locked` to completion; tasks that touch golden fixtures or the doctor also run `ICETRAY_GOLDEN_WAREHOUSE=file://$PWD/target/golden-warehouse flox activate -- dev/test-golden.sh`.
- Hot-path tasks (1, 2) measure with the bench against `target/bench/after-task12/bench` (same core library as 59592af): key-value warm-hit, cold-hit, miss, multi-get-100 (full/disk/bloom, single), scan-warm, scan-bounded-long, scan-bounded-string, multi-scan-100, and the load scenarios of ordered_rolled and ordered_appended, three alternating runs; allocations per op must be equal or lower, and nothing slower outside the spread.
- Commits: Conventional Commits (`refactor(...)`), no trailers, explicit paths, never bypass the hook, no push. No `#[allow]`.
- Disk is limited: keep 10 GiB free; remove `target/debug/incremental` as needed; never remove `target/golden-warehouse`, `target/bench/tables`, or saved bench binaries.
